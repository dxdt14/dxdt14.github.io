Knowledge distillation transfers learned representations from high-capacity teacher models to more efficient student architectures. This thesis develops an information-theoretic framework for studying its fundamental limits, with particular attention to large language models. It characterizes knowledge distillation as a compression mechanism through rate-distortion theory, analyzes student-model performance using learning theory, and introduces antidistillation as the strategic prevention of knowledge transfer.
